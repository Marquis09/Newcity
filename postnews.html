Exciting News: We are moving!

After five fantastic years of gathering at Monkston Primary School, we are thrilled to announce that starting from 7 September 2025, our Sunday meetings will be held at Oakgrove School.

By God's grace, our church is growing, and we are excited to move to a larger venue with the capacity for further growth. We want to see God's kingdom growing, more people exploring the Christian faith and being baptised into the church.

For a long time, we've been praying for a venue to accommodate our growing needs, and God has kindly answered us by providing Oakgrove School.

There is ample space for parking, a brilliant welcome area that's perfect for mingling over coffee, and plenty of space for our growing children's work.

We're looking forward to welcoming you to our services at Oakgrove School from Sunday 7 September at 10.30am.

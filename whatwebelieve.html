About Us

Inviting everyone to follow Jesus.


Our mission is simple:


Inviting everyone to follow Jesus.


Everything we do flows from that mission. We want people across Milton Keynes to hear the good news of Jesus Christ, put their trust in him, and grow as his disciples.

“He is the one we proclaim, admonishing and teaching everyone with all wisdom, so that we may present everyone fully mature in Christ.”

Colossians 1:28

This verse shapes who we are and what we do.

We proclaim Jesus

Jesus Christ is at the centre of our church.


Whether someone is exploring the Christian faith for the first time or has been following Jesus for many years, we want them to know him more deeply. We believe that forgiveness, hope, purpose and eternal life are found in him alone.

We invite everyone

The gospel is good news for everyone.


Whoever you are, whatever your background, and wherever you are on your journey, you are welcome to come and discover Jesus with us.


We want to be a church where people find genuine friendship, loving community, and the opportunity to explore what it means to follow Christ.

We teach God's Word

We believe that the Bible is God's living and trustworthy Word.


That's why faithful Bible teaching is at the heart of everything we do. As we hear God's Word together, he changes us—helping us know Christ more clearly, love him more deeply, and live for him more faithfully.

We make mature disciples

Following Jesus is a lifelong journey.


Our aim is not simply to fill a room on Sundays, but to help people become mature followers of Christ who love God, serve others, and make disciples themselves.


We encourage one another to grow in faith, obedience, prayer, holiness, and joyful service as we become more like Jesus.

We live for God's glory

Everything we do is ultimately for God.


As we worship together, serve our community, care for one another, and share the good news of Jesus, our prayer is that God would be honoured and glorified in all things.

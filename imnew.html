Your Journey to New City Church

Stepping into a new church for the first time can raise a lot of questions. We want to make your experience as smooth, relaxed, and welcoming as possible from the moment you arrive.

Step 1: Getting Here & Parking

Everything you need to find us easily on a Sunday morning.

Where We Meet

Oakgrove School, Venturer Gate, Milton Keynes, MK10 9JQ. Accessible via local bus routes.

Open Map Directions

Free Parking

Plenty of free parking is available right outside the school gates. Friendly team members will be there to greet you.

Service Times

Join us on a Sunday morning for our main gathering featuring singing, prayer, Bible reading, and teaching.

Step 2: Walking Through the Doors

A glimpse into what our Sunday morning gatherings look and feel like.

No Dress Code

Come as you are! Some wear casual t-shirts and jeans, others dress up slightly. You will fit right in regardless.

Families & Children

Kids are treasured here. They can stay for the service or join our fun kids' groups partway through. Noise is never a problem!

Coffee & Connection

Service includes singing, prayer, and Bible teaching. Afterwards, stick around for tea, coffee, and a friendly chat!

Need live translation during the service?

We offer live translation options so you can follow along comfortably in your preferred language directly on your mobile device.

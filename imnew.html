Welcome to New City Church

We’d love you to join us in following Jesus. Everyone is welcome. We've put together this guide to help you know exactly what to expect this Sunday.


Watch: What to expect on Sunday

Take a quick 2-minute tour to see what our gatherings look like before you arrive.

Location

We meet on Sundays at Oakgrove School, Venturer Gate, Milton Keynes, MK10 9JQ. There are nearby bus routes.

Open Directions

Parking

There is lots of free parking available directly at the school. Our team will guide you right in.

The Best Place to Start?

Join us on a Sunday morning—our main gathering. Our service is filled with singing, prayer, Bible reading, and teaching. Please stay for tea and coffee afterwards to chat!

What to Expect

"Let the message of Christ dwell among you richly as you teach and admonish one another with all wisdom through psalms, hymns, and songs from the Spirit, singing to God with gratitude in your hearts.” — COLOSSIANS 3:16

Is there a dress code?

There’s no dress code. We’re pretty informal. You’ll find some people in shorts, t-shirts, and flip-flops, others in suits, and plenty in between. Come as you are.

What’s available for the kids?

Kids are very welcome at all our services and we don’t mind the noise they make. They can stay in for the whole service or join the kids group part way through—whatever they prefer to do.

"Jesus called the children to him and said, 'Let the little children come to me...'" — Luke 18:16

What We Believe

We are part of the Fellowship of Independent Evangelical Churches (FIEC), and so we subscribe to their Doctrinal Basis, which outlines what we believe.
